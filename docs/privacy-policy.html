Privacy Policy

CyberGuard Academy
Effective date: 2026-11-20 · Policy version 1.0

CyberGuard Academy (“the App”) is developed by Abinet Endale (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the App does with your information — and, more importantly, what it does not do.

Short version: The App collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access. Everything runs on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Account credentials (the App has no accounts)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS, or calendar data
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, MAC address)
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • Browsing history or search history

2. Files You Choose to Analyze

The App includes an APK Analysis Lab feature. When you tap “Analyze a Sample APK,” the Android system file picker opens and you choose a file yourself. The App then:

  • Copies the selected file to the App’s private cache directory
  • Reads the file’s contents on your device to demonstrate heuristic analysis
  • Displays the results in an educational format with explanations
  • Deletes the temporary copy when the session ends

The file is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared anywhere. No part of the analyzed file leaves your device.

3. Permissions

The App requests zero Android permissions. Its manifest contains no <uses-permission> entries. The file picker used by the APK Analysis Lab relies on Android’s Storage Access Framework, which does not require any permission and grants the App access only to the single file you explicitly select.

4. Network Access

The App does not request the INTERNET permission and does not make any network requests. It works entirely offline. No data of any kind is transmitted to us or to any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising libraries, no analytics frameworks, no crash reporters, and no tracking components of any kind.

6. Local Data Storage

The App stores a small amount of non-personal information locally on your device using Android’s SharedPreferences:

  • Which lessons you have marked as completed
  • Your quiz score (correct answers / total answers)
  • A flag indicating whether you have dismissed the onboarding screen

This data stays on your device. It is not backed up to any server. You can clear it at any time from Settings → Reset Learning Progress, or by uninstalling the App.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, the App complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

8. Your Rights

Because the App collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, and similar), contact us at the email below.

9. Educational Nature of the App

CyberGuard Academy is an educational app. It does not scan, monitor, clean, or protect your device. Any analysis it displays is for learning purposes only. A “match” reported by the App is not proof of malice, and a “clean” result is not proof of safety.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL you are reading now, with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app’s Play Store release notes.
